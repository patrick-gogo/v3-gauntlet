Words

  • Lap: one batch, from GO to handoff.
  • Round: one trip from building to the exit pair. Change requests start a new round.
  • Wave: every branch merged and deployed to the stack once.
  • Run: one pass of the Playwright matrix, about 14 minutes.
  • Exit pair: two green runs in a row on the same build.

Ruling levels

  • T1 obvious fix (text, typo, CSS nudge): the loop decides.
  • T2 a choice the shop makes (required or optional, limits, defaults): goes ahead with a pick, flagged for the PM before push.
  • T3 business decision (new feature, money, tax): flagged for a full talk with the PM.
  • Unsure between T1 and T2? Treat it as T2.

House rules in the space

  • One heavy thing at a time: builds, the matrix and pytest take turns.
  • Check the stack is free first: pgrep -f "playwright test|exit-pair|deploy-api|build-next"
  • Anything over 10 minutes runs detached.
  • Unclear? Write a ruling and keep going.

Spike checklist

Once you're in your devbox space
  • Does docs/gauntlet/ reach the job uncommitted, and stay out of commits?
  • Can a job read a token from the space and reach the Notion API?
  • The build rules use superpowers skills: inline them in RULES.md, or are they installed in the space?
  • Can a job push and use Jira on "push" day? (tokens in the space)
  • Which permission mode do jobs run in?
